# S2.7 Groupings: flat by recent, and the parent tree

## Requirement

> so one goruping is for example all of them flat shorted by recent / One could be to group it by like the parents, etc...

Zach, Daily Note - Nov 24 2026, # Vibe tracks. Quoted exactly. [OBJECTIVES.md §4](../OBJECTIVES.md#4-the-spec) lists every item; this note
explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

Group by None + Sort by Last updated lists every track flat, newest activity first, no indentation. Group by
Parent shows the tree, children indented under their parent with a fold chevron. Group by State groups under the
state words. Pinned stays on top in every grouping.

## Why

His two named groupings. The flat one answers "what moved today"; the tree answers "where does this sit".

## How it is checked

`S2.7 *`: on fixture boxes with known activity times: flat order = activity order, every row at one indent;
Parent = the tree from Vibe Tracks' file; switching back and forth keeps the selection.
