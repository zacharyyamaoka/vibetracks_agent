# S2.11 Search: a palette on Ctrl+K, Ctrl+O and Ctrl+P that finds text in the tracks' files, the open track first

## Requirement

> pleae support a search bar … bind it also to the hot keys ctrl K ctrl O and ctrk P for now … my goal is to be abel to like quickly jump to any section in the app with that text. Priority should be given to the open vibe track. My use case was that I wanted to see where else it spoke about like ruler v1 so I wanted to search the files for ruler v1.

Zach, 2026-11-25, with a screenshot of the Claude desktop app's sidebar ("+ New", "🔍 Search  Ctrl+K", "Artifacts").
Quoted exactly as relayed, its two elisions marked … ([feedback note](../feedback/2026-11-25-search-bar.md)). [OBJECTIVES.md](../OBJECTIVES.md) lists every
item; this note explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

- **A Search row** in the sidebar's top rows, as Claude's: "Search", with **"Ctrl+K"** at the row's right end, below
  New track (Claude's order: New, then Search) and above the tracks (S2.1's list gains it). A click on it opens the
  palette.
- **Ctrl+K, Ctrl+O and Ctrl+P** each open the same search palette, its input focused; **Escape** closes it.
- **Typing finds the text in the tracks' files** (and may find the app's sections too): each hit names its **track,
  file and line**. **The open track's hits come first.**
- **Enter on a hit opens that track on the page that shows it** (the first hit is highlighted as the results come,
  as in Claude's palette).

## Why

Jumping by text is how he moves through his boxes' notes; his own case was every place the files say "ruler v1".

## How it is checked

`S2.11 *`, in the search group ([`ruler/suite/groups/search.mjs`](../ruler/suite/groups/search.mjs)), with real keys and pointer, measured on screen:

- Ctrl+K opens a palette: a visible, focused text input appears (in the page, not in a box's frame); Ctrl+O and
  Ctrl+P do the same; Escape hides it.
- The sidebar shows "Search" with "Ctrl+K" right-aligned in its row (within 32 px of the row's right end), below New
  track and above the first track row; a click on it opens the palette.
- Typing a box's own README line (unique to that box) lists a hit naming that box, `README.md` and the line number.
- With a track open, typing **"ruler v1"** (on line 5 of every fixture box's README) lists hits from at least two
  tracks, and the first is the open track's.
- Typing the README line of the box with no site and pressing Enter opens that track, and the line is on screen in
  the main pane.
