## What it means

Each row: the track's state dot in the leading slot (where Claude puts its icon), the title, then badges on the right
(age of the oldest wait in red/amber, an agent count). A track with sub-tracks is its own folder, as a Notion page with
sub-pages is: there is no separate folder row.

- **On hover, the dot turns into the fold chevron, in the same slot** (same place, same size slot), and turns back
  when the pointer leaves. His reference (the original Vibe Tracks sidebar): the row "BAM Robotics" hovered, a ▾
  chevron button where its dot was, `?` and `···` at the right end.
- **The chevron says what it does**: its tooltip or accessible name reads **"Fold N sub-tracks (← →)"**, and
  **"Unfold N sub-tracks"** when folded, N the track's own sub-tracks (the rows one level under it).
- **A chevron click folds**: every level under the track leaves the screen and the rows below move up; a second
  click unfolds. It never opens the track. **A title click opens** the track and never folds it.
- **← and → fold and unfold** the focused row (WAI-ARIA's tree view keys).
- **A fold survives a reload.**
- **A folded parent still rolls up** its subtree (S3.4): the pip for a blocked sub-track below, and the agents working
  across it.

## Why

Folding is what makes a roll-up readable: a folded parent stands for its whole subtree, and a long tree stays one
screen. His words of Nov 25 settle the old tension (OBJECTIVES.md §10.1, Oct 16's hover chevron against Nov 24's
pixel-perfect Claude): **the hover chevron in the dot's slot is required**, Notion's pattern, and S2.2's pixel parity
with Claude holds everywhere else. A *should* until ruler v3; a *shall* since.
