## What it means

Each row: the leading slot, the title, then on the right the badges (age of the oldest wait in red/amber, an agent
count) and the track's status at the row's right end. A track with sub-tracks is its own folder, as a Notion page
with sub-pages is: there is no separate folder row.

- **A parent shows its fold chevron left of its title at rest**, in the leading slot, ▸ folded and ▾ open, with no
  hover needed (Obsidian's file explorer). **A leaf shows none**: its leading slot is empty, so titles at one depth
  line up.
- **The status dot sits at the row's right end** (Claude's sidebar draws it there), after the badges; never in the
  leading slot. A paused or grouping row's glyph takes the same place. On hover the row's `···` may take the status
  dot's place, as Claude's does.
- **The chevron says what it does**: its tooltip or accessible name reads **"Fold N sub-tracks (← →)"**, and
  **"Unfold N sub-tracks"** when folded, N the track's own sub-tracks (the rows one level under it).
- **A chevron click folds**: every level under the track leaves the screen and the rows below move up; a second
  click unfolds. It never opens the track. **A title click opens** the track and never folds it.
- **← and → fold and unfold** the focused row (WAI-ARIA's tree view keys).
- **A fold survives a reload.**
- **A folded parent still rolls up** its subtree (S3.4): the pip for a blocked sub-track below, and the agents working
  across it.

## Why

Folding is what makes a roll-up readable: a folded parent stands for its whole subtree, and a long tree stays one
screen. A chevron shown only on hover hid which rows fold until the pointer found them; shown at rest, the tree reads
from the outside. Moving the dot to the right end also matches Claude's own sidebar (the S2.2 reference, Claude
3.6556.1, draws it there), so OBJECTIVES §10.1's old tension (Oct 16's hover chevron against Nov 24's pixel-perfect
Claude) is gone, not traded. A *should* until ruler v3; a *shall* since; its row anatomy changed in ruler v4
([PEP 0004](../docs/peps/0004-ruler-v4-chevron-left-status-right.md)).

## How it is checked

`S2.9 *`, on screen (boxes, computed styles, `elementFromPoint`), with real pointer and key events:

- sidebar group: a plain top-level leaf's round status dot is at its row's right end (its right edge within 40 px of
  the row's) and nothing is left of its title; on the fixture's roll-up parent, with the pointer away from the
  sidebar, a visible control is left of the title (the chevron); a title click opens the track; a chevron click folds
  without opening;
- fold group ([`ruler/suite/groups/fold.mjs`](../ruler/suite/groups/fold.mjs)), on the fixture's package parent, whose sub-tracks go two levels
  deep (one blocked with 2 agents, one working with 1 below it): with the pointer away, a visible fold control is
  left of the parent's title and its round status dot is at the row's right end, and the control is still there
  after a hover and after the pointer leaves; its labels read "Fold 2 sub-tracks (← →)", then "Unfold 2 sub-tracks"
  folded; a chevron click folds both levels and the next row moves up under the parent, a second click puts every row
  back; a title click opens and leaves it unfolded; ← folds and → unfolds the focused row; a fold survives a reload;
  folded, the row still shows the pip for its blocked sub-track and 3 agents.
