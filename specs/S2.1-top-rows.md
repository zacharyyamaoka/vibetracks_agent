# S2.1 Top rows: Home, Review, Feedback, New track, then Search

## Requirement

> On top of that Vibe tracks kidna provides some top level roll up views. / Where we can look at things accross a variety of tracks

Zach, Daily Note - Nov 24 2026, # Vibe tracks. Quoted exactly. [OBJECTIVES.md](../OBJECTIVES.md) lists every item; this note
explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

Above the tracks, in this order: **Home**, **Review**, **Feedback** (with its open count), **New track**, each
with Claude's icon treatment for its top rows, and since ruler v3 **Search** with "Ctrl+K" at its right end, below
New track (Claude's order: New, then Search; Zach, 2026-11-25, [S2.11](S2.11-search.md)). They stay put whatever the grouping below them.

## Why

Already built and in Zach's Nov 24 screenshot; held here so a redesign cannot drop it.

## How it is checked

`S2.1 *`: the four rows exist in this order above the first track row, each one opens its page. The Search row is
checked by S2.11 (its place below New track, its "Ctrl+K", its click), so one missing row is one item not met, not two.
