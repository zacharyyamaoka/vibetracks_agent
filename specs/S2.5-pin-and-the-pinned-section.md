# S2.5 Pin a track; pinned tracks sit in a Pinned section at the top

## Requirement

> with the abiltiy to like pin things and also change how they are grouped

Zach, Daily Note - Nov 24 2026, # Vibe tracks. Quoted exactly. [OBJECTIVES.md §4](../OBJECTIVES.md#4-the-spec) lists every item; this note
explains it and never overrides it.

## What it means

Pin (from the ··· menu, `P`, or a drag into Pinned) moves a track into a **Pinned** section above the rest, with
Claude's foldable section header. Unpin puts it back where it was. Pins survive a restart.

## Why

Claude's sidebar has it and Zach uses it there (his screenshot shows a Pinned section).

## How it is checked

`S2.5 *`: pin two tracks: both under Pinned, in pin order; fold Pinned hides them; unpin returns each to its
old parent and place; all of it survives a `vt serve` restart.
